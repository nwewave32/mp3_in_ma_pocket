# Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 3, 2026

pocket.mp3 ("the App") respects your privacy.

**pocket.mp3 does not collect or transmit any personal information.**
The App has no server, no account, and no login, and it works without an
internet connection. The MP3 files you add and your settings stay on your device.

## 1. Information We Collect

**None.**

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location,
contacts, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or any other personal
information. There is no sign-up. The App does not record or send which tracks
you add or what you listen to.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following is stored **only on your device** to make the App work. It is never
sent to the developer or to anyone else.

| Category | Contents |
|---|---|
| MP3 files | Copies of the MP3 files you choose, kept in the App's own storage so it can play them |
| Playback settings | Shuffle (on or off) and repeat mode, saved only so they stay the same the next time you open the App |

### About your MP3 files

- The App copies only the files you pick. It does not read or index any other
  files on your device.
- Files in the App's storage are **also visible in the iPhone Files app**, where
  you can open, move, or delete them yourself.
- Deleting a track in the App removes the file from your device.
- **They are never uploaded or sent to any server.**

## 3. Permissions

The App does **not ask for any permissions** — no camera, microphone, location,
photos, contacts, or notifications. It uses only the device features below, all
handled within iOS.

| Feature | When and why |
|---|---|
| File picker | Opens the standard iOS file picker when you add MP3 files. The App can reach only the files you pick. |
| Background audio | Keeps music playing while the screen is off or you are using another app. |
| Lock Screen, Control Center, and headphone controls | Shows the current track title and playback time on the Lock Screen and in Control Center, and lets you control playback with your headphones. This information is handed to iOS and never sent anywhere else. |

## 4. Network Use

The App works without an internet connection and has no feature that sends or
receives data.

Tapping "Privacy Policy" in the App opens this page in your device's web browser.
This is the only time using the App involves the internet, and even then the App
passes only this page's address to the browser — no information about you.

## 5. Third Parties and International Transfers

**Nothing is shared with anyone, entrusted to anyone, or transferred to another
country.**

The App bundles no advertising SDK, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no
cookies or similar tracking technologies. We do not sell any information.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

Because nothing is kept on a server, all information exists only on your device
and stays under your control.

- Deleting a track in the App removes its MP3 file from your device.
- **Deleting the App removes all MP3 files you added and your playback settings.**

If you have turned on your device's backup (iCloud Backup, a Mac or PC backup,
and the like), copies of your MP3 files and settings may be included in that
backup. That is a feature you enabled on your device and Apple's policies apply
to it. The App does not send it, and the developer cannot reach it.

> ⚠️ The developer holds no copy. If you delete the App or lose your device, we
> have no way to restore your files for you. Please keep your original MP3 files
> somewhere else as well.

## 7. Your Rights

Because the App collects no personal information, there is no request process to
go through — you can see, change, and delete everything yourself on your device.

- **Access** — view your tracks in the App or in the Files app at any time
- **Rectification** — change shuffle and repeat on the playback screen
- **Erasure** — delete a track in the App or the Files app, or delete the App
- **Portability** — copy your MP3 files elsewhere from the Files app

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of any age and collects no personal information
from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

## 9. Security

Since the App neither collects nor transmits personal information, there is no
path for it to leak. Information the App stores sits inside the app storage
provided by iOS. Because your MP3 files are visible in the Files app (see
section 2), another app can open one only if you choose it there yourself.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes because of new laws or new features, the changes and their
effective date will be posted in the App or on this page.

If a future version adds a feature that processes or sends information — for
example, advertising — we will update this policy before that feature becomes
available, tell you what would be processed, and ask for your consent first where
the law requires it.
